How to Play

Explore: Move with WASD/arrow keys, the mobile D-pad, or click/tap the ground.

Battle: Select a glowing island, then answer four-choice questions to lower the boss's HP.

Learn: Wrong answers return later. Read the explanation, then try again.

Progress: Defeat bosses to earn XP, gold islands, trophies, and new paths.

Shortcuts: H = help, M = mute, Esc = close a popup.
