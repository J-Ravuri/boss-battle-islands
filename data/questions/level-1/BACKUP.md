# Level 1 question backup

This folder contains the original 100 English and Maths questions:

- 10 island packs
- 10 questions in each pack
- 100 questions in total

`data/questions/manifest.json` keeps `level-1` active by default, so these are
also the questions currently used by the game.

Do not overwrite these files when adding Level 2. Put all spelling content in
the neighbouring `level-2` folder.
